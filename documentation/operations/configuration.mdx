## Relay scan boundaries

The relay starts scanning Sepolia at block `11488800` through `SEPOLIA_SCAN_START_BLOCK`. A fixed lower bound prevents a fresh relay process from missing older unexecuted packets after a restart.

The Stellar watcher uses `STELLAR_SCAN_START_LEDGER` for the equivalent boundary. Choose a ledger at or before the first OApp packet that the relay must process.

## Why source and destination counts can differ

The counters measure different events:

- `sentMessageCount` increases when the source OApp successfully submits a packet.
- `receivedMessageCount` increases only after destination verification, commitment, ordered Endpoint execution, and OApp receipt all succeed.

A count mismatch can therefore mean a packet is in flight, verified but not committed, committed but not executed, blocked by a missing nonce, or permanently failed. Compare GUIDs and pathway nonces rather than assuming the two counters update atomically.
