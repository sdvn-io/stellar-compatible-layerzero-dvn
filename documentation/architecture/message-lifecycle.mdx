## Sepolia to Stellar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send on Sepolia">
    MetaMask calls `sendMessage` on the Sepolia message OApp. Endpoint V2 emits the outbound packet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Observe and verify">
    The relay scans Sepolia `PacketSent` logs from the configured start block, reconstructs the packet header and payload hash, and submits verification through the Stellar DVN path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit on Stellar">
    Once the Stellar Receive ULN reports the payload verifiable, the relay commits verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute on Stellar">
    The Stellar executor calls Endpoint `lz_receive`. Endpoint checks the next inbound nonce and invokes the Stellar OApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store the payload">
    The OApp stores the raw message bytes, GUID, source EID, sender, and received-message count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stellar to Sepolia

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send on Stellar">
    Freighter authorizes `send_message`. The Stellar OApp asks Endpoint V2 to emit the packet for Sepolia EID `40161`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify on Sepolia">
    The relay observes the Stellar packet and submits its verification to Sepolia Receive ULN302 using the configured test verifier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit and execute">
    The Sepolia Endpoint commits the verified packet, then executes `lzReceive` on the Sepolia message OApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store the decoded string">
    The Solidity receiver decodes the payload as a string and updates its source metadata and counters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ordering

LayerZero Endpoint delivery is ordered per pathway. For a tuple of source EID, source sender, and destination receiver, nonce `N + 1` cannot execute before nonce `N`. Verification may happen out of order, but destination execution must close every nonce gap.

This distinction explains why a packet can be successfully sent and verified yet fail destination execution with `InvalidNonce` until its predecessor is delivered.
